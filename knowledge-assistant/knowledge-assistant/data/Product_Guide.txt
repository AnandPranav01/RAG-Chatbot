PRODUCT GUIDE: ANTHRASYNC CLOUD SUITE
Anthrasync Inc.

1. Overview
Anthrasync Cloud Suite is a productivity platform offering document storage,
team collaboration, and task management tools for businesses of all sizes.

2. Subscription Plans
- Starter Plan: Up to 5 users, 50 GB storage, $10/user/month
- Business Plan: Up to 50 users, 500 GB storage, $25/user/month
- Enterprise Plan: Unlimited users, unlimited storage, custom pricing

[PAGE 2]
3. Key Features
- Real-time document collaboration
- Task boards with deadline tracking
- Integration with email and calendar apps
- Role-based access control for team members

4. System Requirements
The web application works on any modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari).
The desktop app is available for Windows 10+ and macOS 11+. A minimum of 4 GB
RAM is recommended for smooth performance.

[PAGE 3]
5. Data Backup
All user data is automatically backed up every 24 hours. Enterprise plan
customers can request backup frequency as low as every 1 hour.

6. Support
Starter and Business plan customers receive email support with a 24-hour
response time. Enterprise customers receive 24/7 priority support with a
dedicated account manager.
