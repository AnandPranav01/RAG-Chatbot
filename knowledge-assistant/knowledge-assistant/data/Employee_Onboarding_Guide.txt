EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING GUIDE
Anthrasync Inc.

1. Welcome
This guide outlines the steps new employees should follow during their first
two weeks at Anthrasync Inc.

2. Day 1 Checklist
- Collect laptop and access card from IT desk
- Complete HR documentation (ID proof, bank details, tax forms)
- Set up company email and Slack account
- Meet your assigned onboarding buddy

[PAGE 2]
3. Week 1 Goals
- Complete mandatory compliance training (Code of Conduct, IT Security,
  Anti-Harassment Policy)
- Set up development environment (for technical roles) or relevant tools
  (for non-technical roles)
- Schedule 1:1 meetings with manager and key team members

4. Probation Period
All new employees undergo a 90-day probation period. Performance will be
reviewed at the 30-day, 60-day, and 90-day marks by the reporting manager.

[PAGE 3]
5. Benefits Enrollment
Employees must enroll in health insurance and other benefit programs within
30 days of joining. Failure to enroll within this window may delay benefit
activation until the next open enrollment period.

6. Probation Confirmation
Upon successful completion of the probation period, employees receive a
confirmation letter and become eligible for full leave benefits as described
in the HR Leave Policy.
