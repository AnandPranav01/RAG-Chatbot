CUSTOMER REFUND POLICY
Anthrasync Inc.

1. Overview
This document describes the refund and return policy applicable to all products
purchased through Anthrasync's online and offline retail channels.

2. Refund Eligibility
Customers are eligible for a full refund if a return request is raised within
30 days of the original purchase date. The product must be unused and returned
in its original packaging.

[PAGE 2]
3. Refund Process
Once a return is received and inspected by our warehouse team, refunds are
processed within 5-7 business days. The refund will be issued to the original
payment method used at the time of purchase.

4. Non-Refundable Items
The following items are not eligible for refund:
- Gift cards
- Downloadable software products
- Items marked as "Final Sale"

5. Damaged or Defective Products
If a product arrives damaged or defective, customers must report the issue
within 48 hours of delivery by contacting customer support. In such cases,
a full refund or replacement will be provided at no additional cost.

[PAGE 3]
6. Exchanges
Exchanges are allowed within 15 days of purchase for a different size or color
of the same product, subject to availability. Exchanges do not extend the
original 30-day refund window.
