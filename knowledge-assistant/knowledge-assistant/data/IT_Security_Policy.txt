IT SECURITY POLICY
Anthrasync Inc.

1. Purpose
This policy defines the minimum security standards employees must follow when
using company devices, accounts, and systems.

2. Password Requirements
All employee accounts must use passwords that are at least 12 characters long,
containing a mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special
characters. Passwords must be changed every 90 days.

[PAGE 2]
3. Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
MFA is mandatory for all employees accessing company email, VPN, and internal
tools. Employees who disable MFA without IT approval will have their access
suspended.

4. Device Security
Company laptops must have disk encryption enabled at all times. Employees must
not install unauthorized software or connect unapproved external storage devices
to company systems.

5. Data Handling
Confidential company data must not be stored on personal devices or personal
cloud storage accounts (e.g., personal Google Drive, Dropbox). All confidential
data must be stored only in approved company systems.

[PAGE 3]
6. Incident Reporting
Any suspected security incident, including phishing emails, lost devices, or
unauthorized access, must be reported to the IT Security team within 1 hour
of discovery via the incident reporting portal.
