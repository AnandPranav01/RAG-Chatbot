HR LEAVE POLICY
Anthrasync Inc.

1. Purpose
This policy outlines the leave entitlements available to all full-time employees
of Anthrasync Inc. It is designed to support employee well-being while ensuring
business continuity.

2. Eligibility
All employees who have completed their probation period (90 days from date of
joining) are eligible for the leave benefits described in this document.
Employees still in probation may take unpaid leave only, subject to manager approval.

[PAGE 2]
3. Types of Leave

3.1 Annual Paid Leave
Employees are eligible for 24 paid leaves annually, credited at the rate of
2 leaves per month. Unused paid leave can be carried forward to the next year,
up to a maximum of 10 days. Any leave beyond this cap will lapse at year end.

3.2 Sick Leave
Employees receive 12 sick leaves per year. A medical certificate is required
for sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days.

3.3 Maternity Leave
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave, in line with
applicable labor laws.

[PAGE 3]
3.4 Paternity Leave
Male employees are entitled to 2 weeks of paid paternity leave, to be availed
within 6 months of the child's birth.

4. Leave Application Process
Employees must apply for leave through the internal HR portal at least 3
working days in advance, except in cases of medical emergency. Leave requests
must be approved by the employee's direct manager.

5. Unapproved Absence
Any absence without prior approval and without valid justification will be
treated as Leave Without Pay (LWP) and may result in disciplinary action.
